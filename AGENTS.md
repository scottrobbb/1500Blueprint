## Product design direction

- Do not add decorative all-caps labels, badges, or eyebrow text with widened letter spacing. Omit purely decorative labels instead of restyling them, and present necessary information in normal title or sentence case.
- Do not add generic explanatory subtitles beneath self-explanatory settings headings or navigation items. Keep only copy that communicates necessary account, plan, billing, allowance, or feature facts.
